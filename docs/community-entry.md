## Title

**AI- Illustrated Business & Money Documentaries (LedgerFilms Studio)**

## Short description

Make MagnatesMedia-style business and money documentaries for YouTube in VideoExpress with a browser agent. One prompt produces a LONG 16:9 ~10-minute documentary or a SHORT 9:16 45–60 second video: research, script, character bible, cinematic AI-art scenes, narration, motion graphics, trim and export.

## Body

**Format:** LONG 16:9 (~10 min) or SHORT 9:16 (45–60 s). Default: LONG.

**How to use**

1. Sign in to https://app.videoexpress.ai/.
2. Open a browser-capable chat (ChatGPT agent mode with Full access, Claude in Chrome, or the Claude desktop app).
3. Paste the prompt from https://github.com/sayeedbind/ledger-films-studio (SYSTEM_PROMPT.md).
4. Give the format and the story.

**What it does**

- Researches verified public-record facts and writes a hook-first script.
- Builds a verbatim Character Bible and fixed location blocks, then uses + Consistent Character on every shot.
- Generates cinematic AI-illustrated scenes in a fixed teal-and-amber painterly style, motion starting on frame 1.
- Writes one continuous narration with a single calm authoritative narrator voice, music bed under it at about -18 dB.
- Adds lower-thirds, chapter titles, money counters, chart and map reveals on track 4.
- Trims all dead air, runs Auto Align Clips, and exports High / FullHD / MP4 (1920×1080 LONG, 1080×1920 SHORT).

**Notes**

- Rate-limited parallel rendering is handled with retries; a full queue is never a failure.
- Original dramatized work only. No real-person likeness, no on-screen text baked into images, no news-style presentation.
- Exports stay in My Videos for 40 days.
