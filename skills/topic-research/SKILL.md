# Topic research (LedgerFilmsHQ)

Find what is genuinely hot in the business and money niche **right now**, rank it, and hand back production-ready briefs. This skill runs **before** `PLAYBOOK.md`. It ends with a ranked shortlist and three full briefs; the user picks one, then the production playbook produces it.

Full instructions: `references/topic-research-playbook.md`. Source access map and exact URLs: `references/sources.md`.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Live data only.** Every claim of "trending" must come from a source you actually fetched in this session, with a timestamp. Never present memory as current trend data.
2. **Browser and web fetch only.** No code, no shell, no local processes, no API keys. Use the browser for Google Trends and Reddit; use direct HTTP fetch for the RSS feeds.
3. **Cite the source per candidate.** Every candidate topic lists its source(s) and a one-line why-now.
4. **No preflight, no approval gates.** Run the whole research pass, then present. Ask nothing until the shortlist is ready.
5. **Ad-safety gate every candidate.** Kill anything that needs a real-person likeness, a defamatory claim, private data, or a news-style presentation of a real living person.

## Method

1. **Sweep the sources** (parallel where possible): Google Trends trending + a finance/business category view; Google News RSS queries for the niche; CNBC, WSJ, BBC, FT, Bloomberg, TechCrunch RSS; Reddit business/entrepreneur/finance/personalfinance/technology top-of-week.
2. **Extract candidates.** A candidate is a story with a subject, a stake and a turn: a collapse, a rise, a scam, a deal, a reversal, a founder, a fortune made or lost.
3. **Score each** on the six axes in the playbook: Trend heat, Evergreen pull, Ad-intent, Story arc, Availability of public facts, and Shorts potential.
4. **Rank and present 10.** Show the scores and the one-line why-now.
5. **Write 3 full briefs** for the top three: a LONG and a SHORT brief each, with hook, angle, chapters or beats, key facts to verify, visual motifs, and an ad-safety note.

## Only stop for

A sign-in or a captcha on Google Trends or Reddit. Skip the blocked source, note it, and continue with the rest.
