# Cinematography (LedgerFilms)

Apply these to **every** generated clip. Cinematography is what makes AI art read as a documentary instead of a slideshow.

## Shot sizes — rotate, never repeat twice in a row

| Size | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Extreme wide | Scale: skyline, boardroom, factory floor, stadium. |
| Wide | Character in their world; status (corner office, cell, yacht). |
| Medium | Default storytelling shot; face plus gesture. |
| Close-up | Emotion, tension, decision. Use at every reveal. |
| Extreme close-up | Money, signature, shaking hand, falling chart. |
| Insert | The briefcase closing, the unread email, the key. |

## Camera moves — one per shot, with a named destination

Write it as **move + amount + subject + reveal**:

- "slow push-in 10% onto his face, ending on his eyes"
- "slow pull-out to a wide, ending with him alone in the frame"
- "pan left across the boardroom, ending on the empty chair"
- "crane up from the desk to the skyline, ending above the tower"
- "orbit 30 degrees around the car, ending front-on"
- "whip pan right, ending on the rival mid-sentence" (Shorts)
- "rack focus from the cash on the desk to his face"
- "drone rise to reveal the full campus, ending on the logo-free facade"

Rules: one primary move per shot; motion starts on **frame 1**; the move must **end** where you said; never combine two moves in one generation.

## Lighting and lens language

- Low-key key + rim + practicals. Motivate every source (window, lamp, screen, street).
- Shallow depth of field on close-ups; deep focus on establishing shots.
- Match the era: 1980s = warm, grainy, analog; 2010s+ = cool, clean, glassy.
- Weather is emotion: rain = crisis, golden hour = rise, hard noon = exposure, fog = mystery.
- Palette: deep navy/teal shadows, amber highlights, muted gold for money motifs.

## Cutting rhythm

- LONG: new image every ~5 s; hard cuts between chapters; dissolve only for time jumps.
- SHORT: cut every 2–3 s; hard cuts and whip pans; payoff on a hard cut to black for one frame.
- Delete any two shots that show the same thing.

## Composition per ratio

- **16:9:** subjects in the central 80%; keep the lower 12% clear for a possible lower-third.
- **9:16:** faces and money in the central 60% vertical band; the top 14% and bottom 20% are UI-covered — compose nothing important there.
