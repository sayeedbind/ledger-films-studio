# Fast pacing and dead-air trimming (LedgerFilms)

## Pacing math

- **LONG (16:9, ~10 min):** about 1,450 narration words at ~145 wpm. One new image every ~5 s ⇒ about 120 beats. One new camera move every ~10 s.
- **SHORT (9:16, 45–60 s):** about 150 narration words at ~150–160 wpm. About 12–14 shots at 3–4 s.
- **Clip length:** 4 s for b-roll, 5–8 s for narration beats and slow reveals. Motion starts on frame 1.
- Long-form chapter title every 90–150 s. Escalate scale, danger or money every ~40 s.

## Hook formulas (first 3–15 s)

Pick one and open on the most extreme image of the whole story:

- "In [year], [company] was worth [$X billion]. [N] [days/months] later, it was gone."
- "He signed a deal that would either make him a billionaire or end him. He signed it twice."
- "Everyone said it was impossible. [Person] had already done it — [time] before anyone noticed."
- "This building cost [$X]. Its owner never set foot in it again."
- "[$X]. That was the number that killed [company]."

## Words and rhythm

- Banned: "in this video", "let's dive in", "without further ado", "let me know in the comments" (except the final CTA), "literally", "very", "amazing", "game-changer", "in today's world".
- One idea per sentence. Active voice. Present tense for the drama.
- Numbers are the drama. Say the number, then show it as a motion graphic.
- Never repeat a sentence's meaning in the next sentence.

## Dead-air trim procedure

1. For each clip, read the waveform: find the true start of audio/video and the true end.
2. Place the playhead at that boundary, click **Cut**, then **Delete** the silent or frozen fragment.
3. Do this for both the head and the tail of every clip.
4. Close every remaining gap by edge-drag.
5. Run **Auto Align Clips** after all clips are placed and trimmed.
6. Never speed up or slow down audio to hit a runtime. Never add filler to reach a target length. Cut a beat instead.

## Runtime control

- LONG: land between 9:30 and 10:30. If short, add a chapter beat. If long, cut a chapter beat.
- SHORT: land between 45 and 60 s. If long, cut the weakest shot.
