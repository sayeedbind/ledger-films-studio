# Motion graphics (LedgerFilms)

Motion graphics carry the "money" information. Keep them premium, consistent, and on **track 4**.

## Brand motion kit — build once, reuse every video

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| **Lower-third** | Thin gold rule top and bottom; subject name in a clean sans; role beneath. Slide in from the left 0.4 s, hold 3 s, slide out. First introduction of a subject only. |
| **Chapter title** | Dark card, gold rule, chapter number as a small arabic numeral, chapter name centred. Fade in 0.5 s, hold 2 s, fade out 0.4 s. |
| **Money counter** | Big centred number, counts up fast (0.5–0.8 s) with a subtle scale settle. Reveal exactly when the narration says it. |
| **Chart motif** | The recurring rising-then-falling line; draws on in 1 s; the tail turns red and craters at the story's turn. |
| **Map reveal** | Dark world map; one gold pin drops with a soft pulse; a route line draws to the second location. |
| **Pull-quote** | One short narration line, bottom-centre, gold on dark. |

## Rules

- One graphic idea per beat, never two at once.
- Graphics enter on the narration keyword, not before it.
- Stay inside the safe area (see cinematography reference).
- No graphics in the hook frame — the hook image speaks for itself.
- Never cover a face.
- Every on-screen number must match the narration exactly and be defensible. Mark estimates "≈" or "reported".
- Never invent a chart shape that contradicts the story.

## Ratio differences

- **16:9:** lower-thirds bottom-left; chapter cards full-frame.
- **9:16:** larger elements, higher contrast, faster motion. Counters snap rather than count smoothly.

## Subtitles

- **LONG:** optional; small, bottom-centre, plain white with soft shadow; never cover faces.
- **SHORT:** burn in captions, one to three words per chunk, high-contrast, upper-middle or bottom safe area. Captions match the narration word for word.

## Text-in-image rule

Never bake text, company names, numbers or logos into generated images. All text is added later as a motion graphic, or omitted.
